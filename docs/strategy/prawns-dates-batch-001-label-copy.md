## What changed from the earlier draft, and why

1. **Prawns 59% → 35%.** The 59% was fresh market weight over jar weight, which mixes bases. FSSAI 5(2)(g) asks for the ingoing percentage at time of manufacture; the prawn that goes in is the cleaned prawn.
2. **Dates 22% → 23%.** Same basis, rounding.
3. **Gingelly oil moved ahead of vinegar.** Descending order by weight at time of manufacture, with the evaporated water left out (5(2)(f)).
4. **Name → "Prawns & Dates Pickle, oil based".**
5. **"Use by: 03/2027" added.** FSSAI 5(10)(a) makes Expiry/Use by mandatory; Best before is optional and may stay as extra information.
6. **Nutrition: per serve and % RDA columns added.** 5(3) requires per 100 g and per serve with % RDA contribution. Serving size 20 g.
7. **"Incl. of all taxes" added to MRP** per Legal Metrology.
8. **Storage rewritten as full sentences** and the em dash removed from the Malabar Edit copy.
