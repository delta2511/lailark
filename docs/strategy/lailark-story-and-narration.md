# Lailark: the story, and what may be said

Approved 14 Sep 2026. Drawn from the Neduvanchalil Table internal brief of the same
date, filtered down to what belongs to the pickle.

This doc exists so that nobody, human or otherwise, has to decide in the moment what
Lailark is allowed to claim. Section 3 is the part that matters most: the things that
are true and still may not be printed.

---

## 1. The spine

The jar is the two families. Fish from Sumayya's own shore at Chaliyam, spice from the
hill road that comes down from Wayanad.

That is not a story laid over the product. It is a description of what is in the jar,
which is why it holds up under questioning and why a competitor cannot borrow it.

The reason it exists: pickles have been getting quicker and cheaper for years, through
food engineering and price optimisation, and Lailark is keeping the original methods and
ingredients intact instead.

**The food carries the story. Everything else is a footnote.** This is the correction
made on 14 Sep after the first draft led with the land's name: the etymology is
interesting, but it is not why anyone buys a jar. The food, and the family the food came
through, are the story. The name is a note at the end for the one reader in twenty who
wonders what the word on the label means.

## 2. Approved elements, in order of weight

**The two houses, and the cooking.** The family is Mappila. One side is from Chaliyam,
where the river meets the sea. The other is from Kunnamangalam at the foot of Wayanad, on
the old malancharakku road that carried hill produce and spice down to the Kozhikode
port. One side knew fish, the other knew spice.

Sumayya grew up cooking in both. She learned at her own mother's side in Chaliyam and has
been at it for decades, all by hand. None of it was ever written down, and no restaurant
ever bothered to hold on to it.

**Chaliyam is her shore, not a supplier.** This is the single largest upgrade to copy
that already existed. The earlier line said the prawns come in at Chaliyam, a short drive
from the kitchen. The true version is that Chaliyam is where Sumayya's mother's kitchen
was and where she learned. It is not sourcing. It is going home for the fish. Say fish
rather than prawns: the shore supplies all of it, not one SKU.

**Sumayya is Shefin's mother**, not the kitchen staff. Saying it plainly changes how the
handwritten note in every box lands.

**The method, which is the mission.** Pickles and food like this have been getting
quicker and cheaper for years, through food engineering and price optimisation. Lighter
oils, a few shortcuts, a shorter list of spices, until a lot of it stopped tasting the
way it should. Lailark keeps the original methods and ingredients intact, and that is
what it costs to make properly. Condition inherited from the Table: never a rant, never a
paragraph where a sentence will do. Nobody buys a jar of pickle to be lectured.

**No stock, only leftovers.** Anything listed as in stock is what was left over from the
previous batch. This is said plainly on the site, because it is the sentence that makes
the whole two-mode shop make sense.

**One licence, one house.** The FSSAI registration is caterer class on the Neduvanchalil
address, which is why paid meals at the house are covered. So the jar and the table are
not a metaphor for each other. They are the same licence at the same address, cooked by
the same person.

**The name, as a closing note.** Nedu is long, chal is channel. The land was once a
creek, water coming off the hills to the east and finding its way west towards
Kunnamangalam town. The house was built in the water's path and took the land's name
rather than giving it one. Useful because `Neduvanchalil Veedu` is already printed as the
manufacturer on 22 jars, so the address on the label stops being meaningless. Not useful
as an opening, which is why it sits at the bottom of the page.

## 3. Held back, and why

Everything here is true. None of it goes into customer-facing copy.

| Thing | Status |
|---|---|
| **The dates are Sumayya's own specialty** | True, confirmed by Shefin, and deliberately kept out of the copy as of 14 Sep. Do not reinstate it without asking him |
| **Engattil tharavad**, the Chaliyam house by name | Switched off by a Table decision. Lailark is guest-facing, so the same rule applies. It switches on in both places on the same day or the two stories contradict each other in public |
| **Any lineage claim**: generations, centuries, founding dates | Never stated until records back it. This is the single largest gap in the story and the first thing a journalist will ask |
| **A number of years** on Sumayya's cooking | "For decades" is approved, because it is the wording in the canonical note of 23 August. A figure is not, until she confirms one |
| **Dish names** (Pidi, Chera, Ujji kozhi, and the rest) | Not until Sumayya confirms them |
| The Table's **price, seat count, date, booking mechanics** | A different product. Not Lailark's business |
| The Table as a **demand test** before a building | Internal framing only. Publishing it would damage the Table |

## 4. Voice

Inherited from the Table's rules of 23 August, and they now govern Lailark's
customer-facing copy too.

- Warm, sparse, plain.
- **Lailark speaks as "we"**, never first person singular. The Table says "I" and "my
  mother"; Lailark says "we" and "Sumayya".
- Light on dashes. No em dashes at all.
- **No neat parallelism.** No aphoristic endings. A section may simply stop.
- It has to read fully human, with nothing that sounds generated.

Internal docs and working artifacts are exempt. Only what a customer reads gets held to
this.

## 5. Where it landed on the home page

The dark band, which by the design rule carries story while paper carries facts and
anything that takes money, holds the cooking: the two families, Sumayya learning at her
mother's side, and the fish still coming from Chaliyam. Nothing else competes with it.

Order on the page: hero, what is in the kitchen today, the four jars, **the dark band
with the two families and the cooking**, the method, how a batch works, the open batch,
in stock, the batch record, Sumayya's note, the name, footer.

One dark band per page. If a second one ever appears, the page is trying to tell two
stories.
